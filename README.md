# American English IPA sounds

One short recording per sound of American English — 24 consonants, 11 vowels, 5 diphthongs — to play next to an
IPA symbol in pronunciation lessons, and the example words the same speaker says right after each sound. Every file
comes from a source listed below, under that source's license; this README, `credits.json` and the ID3 tags of each
file carry the credit.

**Listen to them all: <https://vocamax.github.io/ipa-audios/>** (GitHub does not play audio inside a README).

The files are cut from recordings of real speakers. Text-to-speech cannot say a sound on its own, and the IPA charts
with audio on Wikimedia use cardinal vowels, not American ones.

## Changes made to the source material

Each file is cut to one sound or word (about 0.1 s of silence before it, 0.2 s after), loudness-normalized to a mean of
−20 dBFS, peak-limited, faded in over 20 ms and out over 60 ms, and encoded as mono MP3 at 96 kbps. Nothing else is
changed. The exact segment of every file is in the tables above and in `credits.json`.

## Using the files

- `sounds/<key>.mp3` — keys are in `sounds.json` (`p`, `th`, `iy`, `er`, `ow`…).
- `words/<key>/<word>.mp3` — example words of a sound, listed under `words.<key>` in `credits.json` (the word as
  written on screen, e.g. `March`; the file name is in lower case).
- `credits.json` — for every file: IPA symbol or word, SHA-1, source title and link (with the time of the segment), author,
  license and whether the source has been verified. Show the credit wherever the sounds are used, or link to this
  README: <https://github.com/vocamax/ipa-audios>.
- With Composer: `composer require vocamax/ipa-audios`, files under `vendor/vocamax/ipa-audios/`. Pin a
  tag: a new tag can replace a file.

## Adding a source

Needs PHP 8.2+ with GD, `ffmpeg`, `ffprobe` and `yt-dlp`.

1. `php bin/scan <youtube url>` — prints the license, every speech segment with suggested cut points and the caption
   words around it, and writes `.cache/youtube-<id>/sheet.png` (a frame from the middle of each segment) and
   `manifest.draft.json`. Videos without a Creative Commons license on YouTube cannot be used without permission.
2. Copy the draft to `sources/youtube-<id>.json` and add one clip per sound: `sound` (key from `sounds.json`), `from`
   and `to` (seconds, padding included) and `label` (what the video shows). Label each segment by the symbol on
   screen in `sheet.png`, not by counting. One file per sound across all sources: move a sound by deleting its clip
   from the old manifest. Example words go in `words`: `sound`, `word` (as written on screen), `from`, `to`, `label`.
3. `php bin/build` — downloads into `.cache/`, cuts, writes `sounds/`, `words/`, `credits.json` and the tables of
   this README. A warning about a large gain means the clip is mostly silence (or a very weak sound such as /θ/, /h/).
4. Listen: `php -S 127.0.0.1:8124 -t .` and open <http://localhost:8124/.cache/preview.html>.
5. `php bin/build --check` before committing. On every push to `main`, `.github/workflows/pages.yml` runs the check
   and publishes the listening page (`php bin/site _site`) to GitHub Pages.

Sources other than YouTube: `"kind": "url"` (direct link to an audio file, e.g. Wikimedia Commons) or
`"kind": "file"` with `"path"` (a recording committed to this repository).

`verified` stays `false` until someone has confirmed that the uploader owns the recording and chose its license; a
`false` source is flagged in the tables and in `credits.json`.
